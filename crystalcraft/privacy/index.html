Crystal Craft: Merge Gems
Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

No Rush publishes Crystal Craft: Merge Gems. This policy explains the iPhone and iPad game's data use and your choices. Contact us at support.norush@gmail.com.

Your choices

Open Settings > Privacy > Manage privacy choices at any time to review regional consent controls or withdraw a previous consent. Rejecting optional ad data use does not purchase No Ads: contextual ads may still appear where your choices and applicable requirements permit them. If required advertising storage or consent is unavailable, affected ad requests remain disabled. The game stays playable when you decline. The separate No Ads purchase removes automatic ad breaks. Live ads remain off until you confirm that you are 18 or older and the required privacy checks permit them. Players under 18, or unsure of their age, can play with ads off. We store the age choice, not a birth date.

Cross-app and cross-website tracking is disabled. The app does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission or use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) for advertising. Ad consent does not enable tracking. Advertising is configured for contextual ads, with personalized advertising and sale or sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising disabled.

Progress and settings on your device

The game saves your current level, tray, remaining drops and time, completed levels, game currency, tutorial progress and preferences on your device so you can resume. Local preferences include privacy and age choices, Game Center sharing, and the No Ads entitlement. No Rush operates no account system or cloud-save server for this game and does not receive your local save. Apple's device backup features may include app data according to your iOS settings.

The game does not request your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location. Separate developer analytics and automatic Unity Analytics collection are disabled. The service processing described below still applies when you use those services.

Advertising and privacy forms

Eligible players can enable contextual advertising through Unity LevelPlay, using ironSource and Unity Ads. To deliver and measure ads, maintain reliability and prevent fraud, these services may process IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, non-advertising app or device identifiers, device model, operating system, language, app information, diagnostics, and ad impressions, views, clicks and reward events. Contextual advertising still involves data processing.

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) provides regional privacy forms and records privacy choices. It may process consent records, IP address and technical information needed to determine applicable messages and deliver the forms. Google is used as the consent provider; this game does not use the Google Mobile Ads SDK to serve ads. The app checks current privacy status before live ad requests. If a required choice cannot be confirmed, ads remain off. Privacy restrictions and your opt-out apply to optional rewarded ads too.

An ad you choose to open may link to an advertiser's website or app. That destination has its own privacy policy.

No Ads purchase

Settings > No Ads offers a one-time App Store purchase that permanently removes automatic ads between levels. Optional rewarded ads remain available when you choose to watch and your privacy choices permit them. Buying No Ads does not change your privacy choices. Restore Purchases checks ownership using your App Store account.

Apple handles payment. Unity In-App Purchasing connects the game to the store. Loading products, checking or restoring ownership, and processing purchases involve product and transaction records. Unity IAP may also process vendor or installation and session identifiers, device and OS information, language or locale, and IP-derived country to operate its purchase service and reporting. Advertising consent is denied for Unity IAP. No Rush does not receive your card details or Apple Account password. Purchase records held by Apple or Unity follow their policies and applicable financial record requirements.

Optional Game Center

Settings > Game Center lets you connect using Apple's Game Center service. When enabled and connected, the game submits the number of levels you have completed to its leaderboard. Apple associates this score with your Game Center account; your nickname and score may be visible to other players. Apple processes account and game activity under its Game Center privacy policy. The app may display your Game Center nickname while connected and does not send it to advertising services.

Select “Turn off Game Center” to stop future score submissions from this game. This does not remove scores already held by Apple or sign you out of your device's Apple Account. Manage your profile, sharing and Apple-held records through Game Center in iOS Settings or Apple. Local level saving works without Game Center.

Support and your rights

If you email support.norush@gmail.com, we receive your email address, your message and any information you choose to include. We use this information to reply, resolve the issue, and meet applicable obligations. Email is handled through our email provider. Do not send payment card numbers or passwords.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of personal data, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, and complain to a data protection authority. Email us to exercise these rights or ask a privacy question. We may need information to identify the relevant record and verify your request. We do not have remote access to your local save or Apple's account records; we can explain which provider can assist with data we do not hold. You may also contact the providers directly through their privacy links below.

Remove the app using iOS “Delete App” to remove its local save and preferences. Offloading an app keeps its documents and data. Manage device backups separately in iOS. Reinstalling may restore backed-up app data; App Store purchases can be restored. Removing the app does not erase records held independently by Apple, Unity, Google or advertisers.

Purposes, retention and security

We use necessary local data to provide the game and remember your choices. We rely on consent where required for optional advertising and device storage, your choice to use Game Center and purchases, and applicable grounds such as providing requested services, legitimate interests in support and security, or legal obligations. You can stop optional sharing without losing local gameplay.

Local game data remains until it is removed from the device or replaced by later progress. We keep support correspondence only as long as needed to handle the request and relevant legal or dispute requirements. Providers retain their own service, consent, transaction, security and advertising records under their policies and legal obligations. They may process data outside your country and describe applicable safeguards in their notices. We use reasonable measures to protect information we handle; no method of storage or transmission guarantees complete security.

This game is not designed to collect children's personal information. Live advertising is restricted to players who confirm they are 18 or older. Parents can manage App Store purchases and Game Center through Apple's parental controls. Contact us if you believe a child has supplied personal information to us.
