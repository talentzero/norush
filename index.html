Privacy Policy — Sweet Sweet Go

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Sweet Sweet Go (the “Game”) is published by No Rush. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the Game and how that information is used.

Information collected

The Game does not require an account and does not ask you to directly provide your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or similar personal information.

The Game uses third-party services for analytics and advertising. These services may automatically process technical and usage information such as:

  • device and operating-system information;
  • app version and session information;
  • gameplay and interaction data;
  • advertising identifiers where permitted;
  • approximate location derived from network or device signals where permitted; and
  • diagnostic, crash, and performance information.

Analytics

The Game uses Unity Analytics to understand how players use the Game. This may include information such as sessions, retention, run duration, progress, gameplay events, and feature interactions.

Analytics are used to improve gameplay, balance, performance, reliability, and the overall player experience.

Advertising

The Game uses Unity LevelPlay, including advertising services such as ironSource Ads and Unity Ads, to display advertisements.

Advertising providers may process device identifiers and other technical information to deliver, measure, limit, and help prevent fraud related to advertising. Depending on your location and consent choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

Rewarded advertisements are optional and may provide an in-game benefit when completed. The Game may also display interstitial advertisements.

Consent and privacy choices

Where required by law, the Game will request consent before enabling analytics and/or personalized advertising. You may be able to change or withdraw privacy choices through the Game’s privacy settings where available.

Children

Sweet Sweet Go is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not specifically directed at children under 13.

Data sharing

Data may be processed by third-party service providers used by the Game, including Unity Technologies and its advertising partners, for the purposes described above. No Rush does not directly sell personal information.

Data retention and deletion

Third-party service providers may retain data according to their own privacy policies, legal obligations, and technical requirements. Where applicable, you may request access to or deletion of data associated with the Game by contacting us at the address below. Some privacy and deletion requests may also be handled through Unity’s privacy tools.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed through the Game and its service providers. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the Game, its features, or its third-party services change. The updated version will be published at this same location with a revised “Last updated” date.
