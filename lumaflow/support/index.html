Puzzles and progress

Press and hold a bubble to squeeze its newest liquid layer. Tap clamps to choose a route and double-tap a bubble to stir. Hints can help when a board has you thinking. Your puzzle progress is saved locally on the device; platform game-profile sign-in does not provide cloud progress synchronization.

No Interruptions and restoring purchases

The optional one-time No Interruptions purchase removes automatic ad breaks. Rewarded videos for hints or extra moves remain optional. Purchases are handled by Apple App Store or Google Play. If ownership is missing, check that you are signed into the store account used for the purchase, connect to the internet, and use Restore in the Game's Settings. Refund requests are handled by the store where you purchased.

Ads and privacy choices

Ads may be unavailable based on age and privacy choices, connection, or available inventory. The under-18 and unsure age routes do not enable live ads. Available privacy options can be revisited in Settings. On iOS, tracking permission can also be managed in system Settings.

Comfort and accessibility

Motion, haptics, music, and sound can be adjusted independently in Settings. The Game includes localized text in English, French, Italian, German, Spanish, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Brazilian Portuguese, and Russian.

Reporting a problem

When emailing support, include your platform, device model, game version, level number, and a short description of what happened. A screenshot can help, but avoid including passwords, payment-card information, or other unnecessary personal information.

Privacy and local data

Read our Privacy Policy for information about game data, platform services, ads, purchases, and privacy requests. Settings → Data & Privacy includes local progress-reset and game-data deletion options. These do not delete platform accounts or store purchases.
