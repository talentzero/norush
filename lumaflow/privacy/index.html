Luma Flow Privacy Policy

Published by No Rush · Last updated: October 14, 2026

This policy explains how Luma Flow (the Game), published by No Rush, handles information. It covers the Android closed-test version 0.1.0 and the features described below. Contact us at support.norush@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests.

Game data stored on your device

The Game stores play statistics, tutorial learning, settings, and privacy choices locally. This version has no developer-managed player account, cloud-save backend, or developer-operated leaderboard. Connecting a platform account does not synchronize your puzzle progress.

Motion effects use device motion sensors to respond to tilting and shaking. These readings are used locally for gameplay and are not uploaded by our game code. Haptic feedback uses the device vibration feature. Motion and haptic feedback can be disabled in Settings. The Game does not request your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location.

Google Play Games

The Android version integrates Google Play Games. The Game checks whether your platform game profile is connected, and Settings offers a connection option. When connected, the Game reads your gamer display name to show your connection status. The Game does not receive your Google password or request your email address or friends list.

Google operates the platform account and may process gamer identity, service interactions, technical information, SDK analytics, and diagnostics to provide and improve Play Games services. The service encrypts its transmitted data using HTTPS. Manage platform profile settings and delete platform game data through Google Play Games or your Google Account. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's instructions for deleting Play Games data. The puzzle game can be played without a connected platform profile.

Advertising, purchases, and analytics

The Game includes an optional No Interruptions purchase, designed to remove interruptions from advertising. This feature is declared as an in-app purchase in the store's content rating. Store availability depends on the release and configuration described below.

Real advertising and in-app purchases are disabled in this closed-test release. The Game does not initialize its live ad-serving or purchasing flows. Editor simulation is a development tool and does not charge players or contact ad networks. Unity Analytics is not enabled by our game code, and our game code denies optional Unity analytics and advertising data consent.

The build includes third-party components for Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform, Unity LevelPlay and its advertising adapters, and Unity purchasing. Google documents automatic SDK collection and sharing of IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. These technical SDK practices are included in our store disclosures even though live ad-serving is disabled. Google states that its SDK transfers are encrypted. See Google Mobile Ads data disclosure, Google's Privacy Policy, and Unity's Privacy Policy. If real ads or purchases are enabled in a later release, we will update this policy and the store disclosures to explain the active providers, data, and available choices before distributing that release.

The Game saves an age-choice preference locally when a player chooses one in Settings. No exact birth date is requested. In the implemented advertising controls, players under 18 or unsure of their age are not eligible for live ads. An age choice alone does not grant advertising or tracking consent.

Support, use, and disclosure

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any information you choose to send. We use it to answer questions, investigate problems, or handle privacy requests. Please send only information needed for your request. We do not directly sell personal information. Information may be handled by service providers to operate the relevant service, or disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect rights and security.

Retention and deletion

Local game information remains on your device until reset, removed with the app's data, or otherwise removed by your device. Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete Game Data returns the current puzzle to Level 1 and removes local play statistics and the four settings preferences as described in the confirmation screen. That action does not delete Google platform data, platform accounts, or separately retained privacy choices. Android app-storage controls can clear the app's stored local data.

To request access, correction, or deletion of information held by No Rush, email support.norush@gmail.com and identify Luma Flow. We may request enough information to verify and fulfil your request. We keep support correspondence only as needed to handle the matter and any legal or security obligations. Google and other service providers control their own retention and deletion processes. Contacting No Rush does not delete your Google Account or Play Games profile.

Children and privacy choices

Children should use the Game with a parent or guardian's guidance and follow the age rules of any connected platform service. No Rush does not ask children to submit personal information through gameplay. If you believe a child sent personal information to our support contact, contact us so we can address it.

Security and changes

We use reasonable safeguards for information we handle, but no electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal information, and to raise a concern with your data-protection authority. Contact support to exercise applicable rights. We will update this page when the Game's data practices change.
