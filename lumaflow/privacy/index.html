Luma Flow Privacy Policy

Published by No Rush · Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy explains how Luma Flow (the Game), published by No Rush, handles information in its Android and iOS releases, including version 1.0 with advertising and in-app purchases enabled. Contact support.norush@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

Game data stored on your device

The Game stores puzzle progress, tutorial learning, settings, privacy choices, purchase ownership information, and optional gameplay statistics locally. It has no developer-managed player account, cloud-save backend, or developer-operated leaderboard. Connecting a platform account does not synchronize puzzle progress.

Gameplay statistics are optional, off by default, and available only after an adult age choice and a separate statistics opt-in. These statistics are used locally; this version has no remote gameplay-analytics service. Turning the option off stops further optional statistics recording.

Motion effects use device motion sensors locally for tilting and shaking. Our game code does not upload these sensor readings. Haptic feedback uses device vibration. Motion, haptics, music, and sound can be changed in Settings. The Game does not request access to contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location.

Platform game accounts

Android integrates Google Play Games and iOS integrates Apple Game Center. The Game checks connection status and offers a connection option in Settings. When connected, it can display your platform gamer name. Our game code does not receive your platform password, email address, or friends list. The puzzles can be played without a connected platform game profile.

Google and Apple operate these services and may process account identifiers, service interactions, technical information, and diagnostics under their own policies. Manage platform game data and account privacy through your platform account. See Google's Privacy Policy, Apple's Privacy Policy, and Google's instructions for deleting Play Games data.

Advertising and privacy choices

The Game supports optional rewarded videos for hints or extra moves and occasional automatic ad breaks between levels. The optional No Interruptions purchase removes automatic ad breaks; rewarded videos remain optional. Ad availability depends on the release, network availability, and the applicable privacy choices.

Advertising is mediated through Unity LevelPlay. The build includes ironSource Ads, Unity Ads, and Google Mobile Ads components; the networks that can serve an ad depend on the mediation configuration and available inventory. These providers may process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, ad interactions, diagnostics, and related technical information to deliver, measure, secure, and prevent fraud in their services.

The Game asks for an age-range choice without collecting an exact birth date. Players who select under 18 or unsure are not eligible for the live advertising route. An adult age choice alone does not grant advertising consent, tracking permission, or optional statistics consent.

Where required, Google User Messaging Platform presents privacy choices before the Game initializes its live advertising route. You can revisit available advertising privacy options in Settings. The Game does not treat a declined tracking request or a declined statistics option as permission for personalized advertising. A privacy choice that does not permit the required advertising storage can leave ads unavailable.

On iOS, an App Tracking Transparency request may be presented when applicable. You can decline it and continue playing. You can manage tracking permission in iOS Settings. The advertising SDKs remain subject to the platform's tracking status and applicable consent signals.

See Google Mobile Ads data disclosure, Google's Privacy Policy, and Unity's Privacy Policy for provider practices. Optional Unity gameplay analytics and Unity IAP developer-data collection are not enabled by our game code. Ad-service measurement and diagnostics are separate from the Game's optional local statistics.

In-app purchases

Apple App Store or Google Play processes purchases and payment details. No Rush does not receive your payment-card information. The Game and its Unity purchasing integration use product, transaction, and ownership information to process purchases, handle pending transactions, restore ownership, and prevent repeat purchase of the same permanent entitlement. Store and platform providers process related data under their own policies.

No Interruptions is a one-time purchase associated with the relevant store account. Use Restore in Settings when needed. Deleting local game data does not cancel a store purchase or delete store transaction records. Manage purchases and refund requests through the store where you purchased.

Support, use, and disclosure

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any information you choose to send. We use it to answer questions, investigate problems, or handle privacy requests. Please send only information needed for your request. We do not directly sell personal information. Service providers may handle information to operate their services, and information may be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect rights and security.

Retention and deletion

Local game information remains until reset, cleared using device storage controls, removed with the app's data, or otherwise removed by your device. Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete Game Data resets puzzle progress and tutorial learning, removes local gameplay statistics, and resets the four game-settings preferences described in its confirmation. It does not delete platform accounts, store transactions or entitlements, separately retained privacy choices, or ad-pacing records.

To request access, correction, or deletion of information held by No Rush, email support.norush@gmail.com and identify Luma Flow. We may request enough information to verify and fulfil the request. We keep support correspondence only as needed to handle the matter and any legal or security obligations. Providers control their own retention and deletion processes; contacting No Rush does not delete an Apple or Google account.

Children and privacy choices

Children should use the Game with a parent or guardian's guidance and follow the age rules of any connected platform service. No Rush does not ask children to submit personal information through gameplay. The under-18 and unsure age routes do not enable live ads or optional gameplay statistics. If you believe a child sent personal information to support, contact us so we can address it.

Security and changes

We use reasonable safeguards, but no electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal information, and to raise a concern with your data-protection authority. Contact support to exercise applicable rights. We update this page when the Game's data practices change.
