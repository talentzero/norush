Luma Flow: request data deletion

Published by No Rush. Last updated: October 14, 2026.

1. Information held by No Rush

Email support.norush@gmail.com with the subject “Luma Flow data deletion”. Tell us what information you want deleted. You do not need to install the Game or create an account. We may ask for information needed to verify and fulfil the request. Do not send passwords.

This version has no No Rush player-account database or cloud-save backend. Information we may hold is support correspondence and information you sent to support. We delete identifiable support information when no longer needed for your request, except information that must be retained for legal or security obligations. We will explain any applicable exception when handling your request.

2. Local game data

In the Game, open Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete Game Data and confirm. This returns the current puzzle to Level 1 and removes local play statistics and the four settings preferences. It preserves tutorial learning, age and consent choices, store entitlement, ad pacing, and platform accounts. To clear all locally stored app information, use Android Settings → Apps → Luma Flow → Storage → Clear data. Android menu names may vary.

3. Google Play Games data

No Rush cannot delete your Google Account or Play Games profile. To remove this game's Google Play Games data, follow Google's Play Games deletion instructions. Google controls retention of its platform data. Deleting Google Play Games data does not erase the Game's local device data.
